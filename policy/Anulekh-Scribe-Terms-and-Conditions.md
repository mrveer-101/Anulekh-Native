# **Anulekh — Terms & Conditions for Scribes** 

_A scribe for every voice.  ·  Draft v0.1 · Working document · Not legal advice_ 

These Terms & Conditions govern your participation on Anulekh as a Scribe. A scribe is a volunteer who writes an examination on a student's dictation. By registering as a Scribe you accept these Terms, which should be read together with the shared Anulekh Guidelines. 

Being a scribe is an act of service, and it carries real responsibility. Two duties in particular — writing only what is dictated (Section 5) and protecting the students you work with, especially minors (Section 7) — are the ones whose breach causes the most serious harm. Please read them carefully. Nothing in this document is legal advice, and the final word on any examination always rests with the examination authority. 

## **1. The non-negotiables** 

Everything below elaborates on six rules Anulekh will never compromise on: 

**1.** A scribe writes only what is dictated — no prompting, hinting, explaining, correcting, or contributing. 

**2.** No money changes hands. Scribes are never paid by students, and no gift or favour may be solicited or accepted. 

**3.** Every user is identity-verified. You will not be deployed to an unverified student, and you must complete verification yourself. 

**4.** Minors are protected first. Safeguarding overrides convenience every time. 

**5.** Personal and disability data is confidential. 

**6.** The examination authority has the final word. 

## **2. Definitions** 

- **Scribe —** you: a volunteer who writes an examination on a student's dictation. 

- **Student —** a person with a disability (or a guardian acting for them) seeking a scribe. 

- **Guardian —** a parent or legal guardian of a student who is a minor. 

- **Minor —** any person under 18 years of age. 

- **Benchmark disability —** a disability of 40% or more under Section 2(r) of the RPwD Act, 2016. 

- **Examination authority —** the board, commission, university, or body conducting the examination. 

- **Anulekh / the platform —** the Anulekh service and the team operating it. 

## **3. Eligibility & verification** 

### **3.1 Identity verification is mandatory** 

You must complete identity verification (upload of a government-issued ID) before you are eligible for any match. This step cannot be skipped. The non-blocking version used in the prototype does not carry over to the live platform.

## **4. The scribe's role — the core rule** 

#### **The whole of your role** 

You read the question as written. You write the answer as spoken. You read it back on request. That is everything. When in doubt, do less, not more. 

This is the single most important operational rule on the platform, and the one whose violation causes the most serious harm — to the student (disqualification), to Anulekh (loss of institutional trust), and to the wider cause. You write exactly what the student dictates — no more, no less. 

Specifically, you must NOT: 

- suggest, hint at, or supply any part of an answer; 

- explain, interpret, paraphrase, or comment on a question beyond reading it aloud as written; 

- indicate whether an answer is right, wrong, complete, or incomplete; 

- correct spelling, grammar, facts, or reasoning unless the student explicitly dictates the correction; 

- alter the order, emphasis, or content of what is dictated; 

- carry any note, device, or material into the examination that the authority has not permitted. 

Any breach of this section is treated as the most serious category of misconduct. It results in immediate removal from the platform, in addition to any consequence the examination authority imposes. 

## **5. No financial dealings** 

Anulekh runs on volunteered time, and its integrity depends on keeping it that way. This rule also keeps Anulekh clearly distinct from a paid-scribe marketplace, which many examination authorities prohibit. 

- You provide your service free of charge. No fee, wage, honorarium, or “reimbursement” beyond what an examination authority itself officially provides may be sought or accepted from a student. 

- You must not use the platform to advertise, solicit, or arrange any paid service, tutoring, or transaction. 

- You must not accept gifts or favours offered in exchange for a match, preferred treatment, or improved service. 

Any attempt to turn a match into a paid or quid-pro-quo arrangement — in either direction — is grounds for removal.

## **6. Code of conduct** 

- **Respect.** Treat every user with courtesy. No harassment, abuse, intimidation, or demeaning language. 

- **Non-discrimination.** Never treat anyone less favourably on the basis of gender, religion, caste, region, language, disability type, or any similar ground. 

- **Reliability.** Honour your commitments. If you cannot make an examination, give as much notice as possible — a no-show can cost a student their exam. 

- **No proselytising or personal agendas.** The examination is the only purpose of the interaction. 

- **Honesty.** Provide accurate information about yourself, your eligibility, and your circumstances. 

- **Accessibility-first communication.** Follow the student's stated preferences for how they wish to communicate and be assisted. 

## **7. Child protection & safeguarding** 

Anulekh connects students — many of them minors — with scribes who may be adults and strangers. Safeguarding is not one section among many; it is the frame around all of them. Where a student is a minor, these rules override convenience every time. 

- **Guardian consent.** Never work with a minor without a guardian's recorded consent. Guardians may review the match and are kept informed. 

- **No private isolation.** Communicate with a minor only through the platform's own channels, not private ones, and in a way a guardian can be looped into. Never push a minor to move the conversation off-platform, exchange personal contact details unnecessarily, or meet outside the legitimate examination context. 

- **No secrecy.** You must never ask a minor to keep any communication, arrangement, or information secret from their guardian or from Anulekh. 

- **Meetings are exam-bound.** You meet the student in connection with an examination, ideally at or around the exam centre, within the structure the authority provides. Anulekh does not facilitate social meetings between adults and minors. 

- **No solicitation of personal information.** Never seek a minor's home address, social media, photographs, or any personal detail beyond what a match requires. 

- **Reporting is mandatory.** If you become aware of any conduct that makes a minor unsafe, report it immediately (Section 10). 

#### **Zero tolerance** 

Any conduct that sexualises, grooms, isolates, or otherwise endangers a minor results in immediate and permanent removal and, where warranted, referral to law enforcement. 

## **8. Confidentiality** 

- You must not disclose the content of any examination, a student's answers, or their performance to anyone. 

- A student's disability status, personal details, and identity are confidential and must not be shared or discussed beyond what a match strictly requires. 

- These obligations continue after the examination and after you leave the platform.

## **9. Data privacy & protection** 

Anulekh handles sensitive personal data — including disability information — under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. As a scribe: 

- your own data (including your ID) is collected only to verify you and operate the service, kept only as long as needed, and never sold; 

- you must treat any student information you encounter as confidential and use it only for the match at hand; 

- you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your own data, and may withdraw consent, subject to legal limits. 

## **10. Compliance with examination authorities & the law** 

Anulekh operates within, and defers to, the legal framework governing scribes in India. 

- **Statutory basis.** Section 17 of the RPwD Act, 2016 provides for scribe assistance and extra time (commonly 20 minutes of compensatory time per hour) for students with disabilities. 

- **Governing guidelines.** Scribe arrangements follow the DEPwD guidelines — including the Office Memoranda of 29.08.2018 and 10.08.2022 and the revised comprehensive guidelines of 01.08.2025 — together with each authority's own rules. 

- **The vetted-pool shift.** The 2025 revision directs examination bodies to build their own vetted scribe pools (within a two-year window) and to phase out the older “own scribe” arrangement, while encouraging assistive technology. Anulekh positions itself as complementary infrastructure to this shift — a tool that helps institutions recruit, verify, coordinate, and manage a vetted pool — never as a substitute for, or a way around, an authority's own process. 

- **The authority decides.** Where an authority provides its own scribe, sets eligibility rules, or requires specific documentation, its rules prevail. Anulekh does not represent that a match satisfies any authority's requirements; confirming that remains the responsibility of the student and the authority.

## **12. Enforcement, suspension & removal** 

Anulekh may warn, suspend, or permanently remove any user who breaches these Terms. 

Breaches of the exam-integrity rule (Section 4), the no-money rule (Section 5), or the child-protection rules (Section 7) lead to immediate removal, without a graduated warning. Removal from Anulekh is separate from, and additional to, any action an examination authority or law-enforcement body may take. Anulekh keeps records of removals to prevent re-registration.
