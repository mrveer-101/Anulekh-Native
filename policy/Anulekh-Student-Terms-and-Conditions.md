# **Anulekh — Terms & Conditions for Students** 

_A scribe for every voice.  ·  Draft v0.1 · Working document · Not legal advice_ 

These Terms & Conditions govern your participation on Anulekh as a Student. Anulekh is a volunteer platform that connects students who have a disability with trained volunteer scribes for examinations. By registering as a Student — or, where you are a minor, through your guardian registering on your behalf — you accept these Terms. They should be read together with the shared Anulekh Guidelines. 

Much of what follows is written to protect you. In particular, the rules on what a scribe may and may not do (Section 4) exist for your benefit: they keep your examination fair and keep you from being disqualified. Nothing in this document is legal advice, and the final word on any examination always rests with the examination authority. 

## **1. The promises Anulekh makes to you** 

**1.** Your scribe writes only what you dictate — nothing added, nothing changed. 

**2.** You will never be asked to pay, tip, or do a favour in return for a scribe. 

**3.** You will only ever be matched with an identity-verified scribe. 

**4.** If you are a minor, your safety and your guardian's involvement come before convenience, every time. 

**5.** Your personal and disability information is kept confidential and used only for the purposes you agreed to. 

**6.** Anulekh never overrides the examination authority — it only helps you connect with a scribe. 

## **2. Definitions** 

- **Student —** you: a person with a disability (or a guardian acting for you) seeking a scribe for an examination. 

- **Scribe —** a volunteer who writes an examination on your dictation. 

- **Guardian —** your parent or legal guardian, if you are a minor. 

- **Minor —** any person under 18 years of age. 

- **Benchmark disability —** a disability of 40% or more as defined under Section 2(r) of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016. 

- **Examination authority —** the board, commission, university, or body conducting your examination. 

- **Anulekh / the platform —** the Anulekh service and the team operating it. 

## **3. Who can register as a student** 

To use Anulekh as a Student, you must: 

- have a disability that creates a genuine limitation in writing; and 

- hold any certification your examination authority requires — for example a disability certificate or UDID card — where it is required. 

If you are a minor, you may only be registered with the involvement of a guardian, and your guardian's consent is recorded before any match is made.

## **4. What your scribe will — and will not — do** 

This is the most important protection Anulekh gives you. Your scribe is your hand, not your mind: they read the question as written, write your answer exactly as you speak it, and read it back when you ask. That is the whole of the role. 

Your scribe must NOT: 

- suggest, hint at, or supply any part of an answer; 

- explain, interpret, or comment on a question beyond reading it aloud as written; 

- tell you whether an answer is right, wrong, complete, or incomplete; 

- correct spelling, grammar, facts, or reasoning — unless you dictate the correction yourself; 

- change the order, emphasis, or content of what you dictate; 

- carry any notes, device, or material into the examination that the authority has not permitted. 

### **If a scribe does any of this** 

Report it through the platform straight away. A scribe who breaks this rule is removed from Anulekh immediately — but the breach can also cost YOU your result if the authority treats it as malpractice. When your scribe offers to “help” beyond writing what you say, the safe answer is a firm no. 

## **5. Your responsibilities** 

- **Be honest.** Give accurate information about your disability, your eligibility, and your circumstances. 

- **Be reliable.** Honour your commitments. If you cannot attend an examination, give as much notice as you can — a last-minute change can leave a volunteer stranded. 

- **Be respectful.** Treat your scribe and the Anulekh team with courtesy. Harassment, abuse, or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated. 

- **Confirm the rules.** Anulekh surfaces the authority's requirements, but you (and the authority) remain responsible for confirming that a particular scribe and arrangement are acceptable for your exam. 

## **6. No money, no favours** 

Anulekh is a volunteer platform, and keeping it free of money is what protects you from exploitation. Therefore: 

- You must not offer payment, gifts, or favours in exchange for a match, a preferred scribe, or better service. 

- You must not use the platform to arrange paid tutoring, coaching, or any other transaction with a scribe. 

Anulekh does not charge you for a match. If a scribe ever asks you for money or a favour, do not pay — report it. 

## **7. If you are a minor — and for guardians** 

Anulekh may connect you with a scribe who is an adult and, at first, a stranger. These rules keep you safe, and they apply to every match involving a minor: 

- **Guardian consent.** No match is made without your guardian's recorded consent. Your guardian may review the match and is kept informed.

- **Everything stays on the platform.** Talk to your scribe through Anulekh's own channels, not private ones. A scribe must never push you to move the conversation “off-platform” or swap personal contact details you don't need to share. 

- **No secrets.** A scribe must never ask you to keep anything secret from your guardian or from Anulekh. If someone asks you to keep a secret, that is itself a reason to tell your guardian. 

- **Meetings are exam-only.** You meet your scribe in connection with the examination — ideally at or around the exam centre. Anulekh does not arrange social meetings. 

- **Your details are yours.** A scribe must not ask for your home address, social media, photographs, or any personal detail a match doesn't require. 

### **Tell someone** 

If anything a scribe says or does makes you uncomfortable or unsafe, tell your guardian and report it on Anulekh immediately. Reports about a minor's safety are treated as urgent and acted on first. 

## **8. Confidentiality** 

Your disability status, your answers, and your examination performance are confidential. Your scribe must not disclose them to anyone, and this obligation continues after the examination is over and after either of you leaves the platform. 

## **9. Your data & privacy** 

Anulekh handles sensitive information — including your disability data, which is treated with particular care — in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. 

- **Consent and purpose.** Your data is collected only with consent and used only to verify identity, make matches, and run the service. 

- **Only what's needed.** Anulekh collects the least data required, and no more. 

- **Children's data.** A minor's data is processed only with verifiable guardian consent. Anulekh does not track, profile, or advertise to minors. 

- **Kept only as long as needed.** Your data is retained only for its purpose, then deleted or anonymised. 

- **Never sold.** Your data is never sold, and not shared with third parties except where you consent or the law requires. 

- **Your rights.** You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and may withdraw consent, subject to legal limits. 

## **10. Examinations and the law** 

Anulekh operates within the legal framework governing scribes in India and defers to it. 

- Section 17 of the RPwD Act, 2016 provides for scribe assistance and extra time (commonly 20 minutes of compensatory time per hour) for students with disabilities. 

- Scribe arrangements follow the DEPwD guidelines — including the Office Memoranda of 29.08.2018 and 10.08.2022 and the revised comprehensive guidelines of 01.08.2025 — together with the specific rules of each examination authority. 

- **The authority decides.** Where an examination authority provides its own scribe, sets eligibility rules, or requires specific documents, its rules prevail. Anulekh does not promise that a match satisfies any authority's requirements — confirming that is your and the authority's responsibility.

## **11. Reporting & grievances** 

You may report any concern — misconduct, a safety worry, a breach of these Terms, or a data-privacy issue — through the platform's reporting channel. Reports about the safety of a minor or about examination integrity are treated as urgent and reviewed immediately. Anulekh will acknowledge your report, investigate fairly, keep your identity confidential where possible, and tell you the outcome.

## **12. Enforcement** 

Anulekh may warn, suspend, or permanently remove any user who breaches these Terms. Removal from Anulekh is separate from, and additional to, any action an examination authority or law-enforcement body may take. Anulekh keeps records of removals to prevent removed users from re-registering. 

## **13. Liability & disclaimers** 

- Anulekh is a facilitation platform. It connects students and scribes; it does not conduct examinations, employ scribes, or guarantee outcomes. 

- Anulekh does not guarantee that a match will be available for any given examination, or that a match will satisfy a particular authority's rules. 

- You are responsible for your own compliance with examination rules and applicable law. 

- Nothing in this document is legal advice. 

## **14. Changes to these Terms** 

These Terms will change as Anulekh grows and as the law evolves. Material changes will be communicated to you, and continued use of the platform means you accept the current version. Each version is dated.
