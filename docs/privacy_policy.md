# Privacy Policy for Anulekh

**Effective Date:** September 16, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 16, 2026

Anulekh ('we', 'our', or 'us') is dedicated to providing an accessible, transparent, and secure digital platform connecting visually impaired and specially-abled students with verified volunteer scribes for academic and competitive examinations across India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Anulekh mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Registration:** When creating an account as a Student or Volunteer Scribe, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and account password.
- **Profile & Eligibility Verification:**
  - *Students:* Academic degree/class, subject details, examination admit card / hall ticket copies, disability type, and disability certificate (used solely to verify exam scribing eligibility under Government of India guidelines).
  - *Scribes:* Educational qualifications, languages known, preferred exam locations/slots, and government-issued photo identity proof (such as Aadhaar card, used solely for identity verification and safety during offline exam visits).
- **Exam Assistance Requests:** Exam name, date, time, subject, examination center location/address, and language preferences.
- **In-App Communications:** Text messages and audio voice notes exchanged directly between matched students and scribes for coordinating exam logistics.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Basic device model, OS version, and network status to ensure compatibility and accessibility support (e.g., TalkBack optimization).
- **Push Notification Tokens:** To deliver instant alerts for scribe applications, match confirmations, and exam reminders.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
1. **Service Delivery:** Matching visually impaired students with suitable, verified volunteer scribes based on exam location, language, and subject.
2. **Safety & Identity Verification:** Verifying that volunteer scribes are authentic and eligible before connecting them with students.
3. **Communication:** Sending real-time status updates, match notifications, and exam reminders via in-app alerts and SMS/WhatsApp.
4. **Accessibility Enhancements:** Ensuring TalkBack, screen readers, and high-contrast accessibility tools operate seamlessly.
5. **Customer Support:** Resolving technical issues, dispute reports, and support tickets submitted through the console.

---

## 3. Data Protection and Security

- All communication between the Anulekh app and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS.
- Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text.
- Identity and disability verification documents are accessible strictly to authorized administrators for verification purposes.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade user personal data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

---

## 4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:
- **Access & Correction:** You can view and update your profile details directly from the Account / Settings tab within the app.
- **Account Deletion:** You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and associated verification files at any time via the Delete Account button in the app settings, or by contacting us at aditya.raj322005@gmail.com. Upon confirmation, all personal profiles, documents, and chat records are purged from our database.
